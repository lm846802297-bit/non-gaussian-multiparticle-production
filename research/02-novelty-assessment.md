# Novelty Assessment and Decision Gate

## Candidate contribution

A controlled calculation of the momentum-resolved outgoing connected two-particle cumulant `kappa4(k,q)` generated dynamically from a Gaussian in-state by a finite-duration interaction in `1+1D lambda phi^4`, together with a direct comparison to the Gaussian/Wick reconstruction from the same final two-point data.

## What is already known

The literature audit establishes substantial prior art in:

- general field-theory relations between real-time correlators and particle spectra;
- Gaussian/Wick two-particle interferometry;
- non-Gaussian initial correlations in Kadanoff-Baym evolution;
- real-time two-point and four-vertex dynamics far from equilibrium;
- perturbative kinetic equations beyond Boltzmann using equal-time four-point correlators;
- nonperturbative MPS particle production and real-time scattering in `phi^4`.

## What remains to be verified

The first-pass search did not locate a work that computes the exact target observable in the same finite-pulse setup. This does not establish that no such work exists. A priority claim requires:

1. forward and backward citation tracing of all papers in `research/01-literature-audit.md`;
2. searches for `out particle number cumulants`, `connected four-mode correlations`, `inclusive two-particle spectrum`, `finite-time coupling quench`, and `non-Gaussian particle production`;
3. inspection of appendices and supplemental material in the 2025 kinetic-theory and 2026 MPS papers;
4. comparison of observable definitions, initial states, pulse protocols, and approximation schemes.

## Go / no-go criteria

**Go** if the exact momentum-resolved connected out-spectrum has not already been computed for an equivalent setup and the calculation can be independently validated.

**No-go / revise** if an equivalent calculation is found. Possible revisions must add a distinct, testable contribution such as a controlled nonperturbative regime, a quantified Gaussian-validity boundary, or a higher-order multiparticle observable.

Do not write “first ever” or “unexplored” in a manuscript until the citation-chain audit is complete.
